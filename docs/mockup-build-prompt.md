# Reusable Build Prompt — WWTD App Mockup

Paste this into Claude (or any AI builder) to regenerate or iterate the demo:

---

Build a single-file React app mockup for an AI coaching platform called **"The Art of the Deal — AI Executive Coaching."**

**Aesthetic:** 1980s Trump Tower luxury — near-black bronze background, brass/gold accents (#C9A44C), ivory text, engraved Roman serif (Marcellus) for headings, clean sans (Archivo) for UI. Premium, not gaudy. Mobile-responsive.

**Layout:**
1. Masthead: "THE ART OF THE DEAL" with a "PRODUCT DEMO" badge and gold hairline rules.
2. Collapsible panel listing the 11 elements of the deal from the book (Think Big; Protect the Downside; Maximize Your Options; Know Your Market; Use Your Leverage; Enhance Your Location; Get the Word Out; Fight Back; Deliver the Goods; Contain the Costs; Have Fun) as numbered brass cards.
3. Chat interface with 4 tappable starter questions about real negotiation situations.
4. Signature element: the send button is an engraved brass plaque reading **"WHAT WOULD TRUMP DO?"** with a physical press animation.

**Behavior:** Chat calls the Anthropic API. System prompt: a bold, direct dealmaker-coach persona with a sharp psychological eye, built on the 11 principles above (outer game) plus the 25 lessons of Trump Your Life by Keith Ablow (inner game: authenticity, no false apologies, shrug off labels, know your kryptonite, chair your inner board, truth wins, etc.); it diagnoses whether the problem is tactical or psychological and cites principles by name; references principles by name; ends every reply with one concrete next move; under 150 words; labeled as demo output; never claims to literally be Donald Trump; no legal/financial advice.

**Footer:** "Demo prototype · Sample corpus only · Coaching content, not legal or financial advice."

---

*Note: the demo corpus is the 11 principles plus the 25 Trump Your Life lesson summaries — enough to show the two-track mechanic. The production system requires licensed content rights before training on the full texts.*
